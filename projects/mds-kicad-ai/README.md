# MDS KiCad AI — Development Milestone and Planned Pilot

**Public development update · 7 November 2026**

Magen Daniel Systems LLP (MDS) has reached an important development milestone in MDS KiCad AI: bringing KiCad-based engineering analysis, a desktop interface and local AI assistance together in an evolving engineering workflow.

Our work builds on an existing implementation. Development and validation are continuing, with the aim of helping students, engineers and companies review electronic designs and work with engineering evidence more efficiently.

## Current Status

MDS KiCad AI is under active development. This announcement marks progress toward a pilot, rather than a completed commercial release. Planned editing and automation capabilities will be introduced progressively as they are validated; this announcement does not promise autonomous PCB or schematic design.

## Planned Pilot

As of this update, MDS is targeting a possible pilot opening in approximately two weeks — around **21 November 2026** — subject to technical readiness and completion of validation. This is an estimate, not a confirmed launch date.

The planned pilot is intended for:

- Students working on electronics and engineering projects.
- Engineers evaluating AI-assisted design-review workflows.
- Companies interested in a limited evaluation in their own engineering context.

Access is planned through a **time-limited paid usage licence, lasting a few months**, under the MDS commercial model. The pilot is expected to carry a **low introductory fee**; it is not planned as a free service.

The exact fee, access period, available capabilities and participation terms will be announced before enrolment. Participants will have an opportunity to evaluate the available workflow and provide feedback during the agreed period.

## MDS Method and Ownership

The proprietary MDS method described in this project belongs to MDS and its inventor, who is the owner of MDS. The method is being developed as an MDS commercial offering.
